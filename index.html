Methodology & interpretation

ROI on incentives = (gross revenue − incentives) ÷ incentives. Incentive cost ratio = incentives ÷ gross revenue. These are incentive-level economics, not net company profitability; funding costs, operating expenses, credit losses and taxes are excluded.
